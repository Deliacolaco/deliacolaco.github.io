About Me

I'm a Business Analytics graduate who turns raw, unstructured data into clear analysis that drives real decisions. The world runs on data that most people find overwhelming - and I found that I didn't. What draws me in is taking something messy and large and turning it into something a real person can actually act on.

That's taken me across very different environments. I built a data collection tool from scratch in Google Sheets - automated checks, documentation, the lot - so the team could keep using it after I left. I managed a retail store where analysing sales data helped close a performance gap from -11.6% to -4.4%. I coordinated professional events end-to-end while finishing a postgraduate degree. I don't wait to be told how - I figure it out, deliver it, and make sure the people around me can do it too.

That same approach carries into my technical work. The projects here span Python, SQL, R, and Qlik Sense - from clustering 2,000 gym members to shape a marketing strategy, to predictive modelling for student retention risk, to building relational databases from scratch. For me, the work is only done when the data has actually moved something.
