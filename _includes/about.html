About Me

I'm a Business Analytics graduate who turns raw, unstructured data into clear analysis that drives real decisions. I got into analytics because the world runs on data that most people find overwhelming, and I found that I didn't. What draws me in is taking something complex and turning it into something a real person can read and act on, not because the visuals look nice, but because of what they say.

That's led to work across very different environments. I built a working data collection tool from scratch in Google Sheets, including automated checks and documentation, so the team could keep using it after I left. I've managed a retail store where analysing sales by category and shift patterns revealed where the gap was actually coming from and helped close it from -11.6% to -4.4% in my first month. I coordinated professional events end-to-end while finishing a postgraduate degree. I don't wait to be told how to do something. I figure it out, deliver it, and make sure the people around me can do it too.

The projects on this site span Python, R, SQL, and Qlik Sense, from segmenting 2,000 gym members by behaviour using clustering techniques to shape a targeted marketing strategy, to modelling which students were at risk of dropping out, to designing relational databases from scratch. For me, the work is only done when the data has actually moved something: a decision, a strategy, or a result.
