description: Analysed four years of Australian retail transaction data across four regions and three product categories to identify margin drag from over-discounting. Built SQL queries to find the exact discount threshold where profit turns negative, and Tableau dashboards for CFO and commercial manager audiences. Key finding — VIC Furniture is the only loss-making segment, running at -1.70% margin with 67.84% of transactions discounted above the break-even threshold.
